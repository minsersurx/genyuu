Imperial Chronicle

From the Founding Records to the Tian Tong Chronology, down to palace secrets never entered into the official archives — the complete history of Genyuu.

Founding Records

In the late Shen Dynasty, the emperor was fatuous and the nobility corrupt. You Ming, a half-god half-demon, orphaned young, was adopted by Dong Jiu, the three-dynasty Prime Minister of the Shen Dynasty, who taught him statecraft. As an adult, You Ming became Commander of the Imperial Guards, aiding Prince Shen Tian's usurpation only to be betrayed, as Shen Tian issued the Demon Slaughter Decree and had Dong Jiu poisoned to death. Despondent, You Ming fled to the Demon Shadow Cave, spent a year merging divine and demonic powers to forge Shadow Flame, and emerged reborn. Allied with Xuan Ming and other old comrades via the Ashen Oath, he turned Shen Ming against the Shen Dynasty and crushed its main force at Dragon Break Ridge, then marched on the capital and executed Shen Tian. After splitting with Heibai Wuchang over ideology, You Ming ascended the throne, founded Genyuu, changed the era to Tian Tong, made Xuandu his capital, and appointed Xuan Ming as Regent. In Tian Tong Year 5, he eliminated the remaining Mohui and Shenying forces, unified the realm, and created a golden age of equality between Gods and Demons.

Empire History

Late Shen Dynasty: You Ming is born, half-God half-Demon; orphaned young, adopted by Dong Jiu, three-dynasty Prime Minister of the Shen Dynasty, who teaches him statecraft.

End of Shen Dynasty: You Ming becomes Imperial Guard Commander, aids Shen Tian's usurpation; Shen Tian issues the Demon Slaughter Decree, Dong Jiu is poisoned to death, You Ming flees to the Demon Shadow Cave.

Nirvana & Emergence: You Ming forges Shadow Flame in the Demon Shadow Cave, emerges to ally with Xuan Ming and other old comrades via the Ashen Oath, and raises an army against the Shen Dynasty.

Battle of Dragon Break Ridge: The Genyuu Army turned Shen Ming against the Shen Dynasty, crushed its main elite force, completely destroying its military power; Shen Ming surrendered to Heibai Wuchang with his remaining troops.

Capture of the Divine Capital: The Genyuu Army marches east in victory, captures the Shen Dynasty's capital, executes Shen Tian, and the Shen Dynasty falls.

Tian Tong Year 1: You Ming ascends the throne, names the empire Genyuu, capital Xuandu, issues the Tian Tong Laws, appoints Xuan Ming as Regent; Heibai Wuchang flees north to found the Mohui Empire, splitting the realm in two.

Tian Tong Year 3: Civil war breaks out in the Mohui Empire; General Shen Ming stages a coup, assassinates Heibai Wuchang, and usurps the throne to found the Shenying Empire.

Tian Tong Year 5: The Emperor personally leads the army, crushes the Shenying Army in the decisive battle at Shadow Burial Plain, Shen Ming dies in defeat, the realm is unified, and the Tian Tong Golden Age begins.

Secret Chronicle

The Ashen Oath: After emerging from seclusion, You Ming secretly gathered Xuan Ming, Heibai Wuchang and other old comrades at a ruined shrine to swear a blood oath: overthrow the Shen Dynasty together, then divide the realm between them. The pact appears nowhere in the official record — only a fragment survives in Xuan Ming's late-life notes.

The Rift: After the capital fell, Heibai Wuchang argued for preserving the old Shen Dynasty hierarchy to placate the nobility, while You Ming insisted on abolishing class altogether. The two clashed openly at the victory banquet and parted ways for good — Heibai Wuchang fled north to found the rival Mohui Empire.

Embers of Mohui and Shenying: After Mohui and Shenying fell, their scattered followers were not hunted down wholesale — the Tian Tong Laws explicitly pardoned commoners and held only the ringleaders accountable. Some who submitted later rose through the Spirit Political Academy's examinations to become the empire's next generation of officials, one hidden reason the Tian Tong Golden Age endured.
