Imperial Chronicle

In the fading days of the Shen Dynasty, You Ming — half-god, half-demon — was raised by Prime Minister Dong Jiu, only to be betrayed and lose his mentor. Reborn in the Demon Shadow Cave, he allied with old comrades, overthrew the tyranny, unified the realm, and founded an empire of equality. That five-year legend, along with palace secrets never entered into the official record, is preserved in the Imperial Chronicle.
