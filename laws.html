Tian Tong Laws

The three foundational laws proclaimed at the Emperor's coronation, the legal bedrock of Genyuu's ideal of equality between Gods and Demons.

Article I: Abolish Class

Erase the divide between Gods and Demons forever; all people are Spirits, equal before the law. This article abolished the Shen Dynasty's old caste system based on bloodline, and was the most far-reaching of the three laws — as well as the direct spark behind You Ming and Heibai Wuchang's falling-out.

Article II: Low Taxes

Implement the 10% fixed tax, ban all surcharges forever; lighten corvée and taxes to let the people recover. Aimed squarely at the crushing levies of the late Shen Dynasty, this article was a key reason the population rallied quickly to Genyuu in the early Tian Tong years.

Article III: Meritocracy

Establish the Spirit Political Academy and schools; appoint officials by talent alone, regardless of origin or race. Implemented by founding dean Mo Chen, this article broke centuries of hereditary privilege and kept the empire supplied with talent, forming the institutional bedrock of the Tian Tong Golden Age's stability.
